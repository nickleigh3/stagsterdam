Day 1

Friday 9 October

01
Arrive · VR · Ceppis · Bar Crawl
The opening gambit
09:55
Depart Luton
easyJet LTN → AMS. Land 12:10. Taxi or train to the hotel — check in from 13:30.
14:00
Lunch — Foodhallen
Amsterdam's best indoor food market. 21 stalls in a converted tram depot. Everyone picks their own — beers at the central bar.
15:30
Free ferry → A'dam VR
Jump the free ferry from behind Central Station. 5-minute crossing to Overhoeksplein. Runs every few minutes.
16:00
A'dam VR — Team Duel 🎮
Overhoeksplein 5. Free-roam zombie shooter + 30 mins F1 racing + 1 drink each. ~1hr. Contact: Rens — 06 12 42 17 11. Pay at venue.
✓ Booked — 8 people, 16:00
18:00
Hotel — freshen up
Back to NH City Centre. Get changed. Pre-drinks at Café Pollux or Eddy Bar if you need a warm-up.
20:00
Dinner — Ceppis 🍕
Lijnbaansgracht 256. Canal-side Italian — burrata, fresh pasta, wood-fired pizza, Italian cocktails.
✓ Booked & confirmed
22:00
Leidseplein bar crawl 🍺
Café Brecht → Café Nol → Bourbon Street → Paradiso → Jimmy Woo. Amsterdam's stag heartland. The Gimp leads the way.
Late
Red Light District 🔴
De Wallen. Peep shows on Oudezijds Achterburgwal. Casa Rosso for the show. No photos near the windows — seriously.

Saturday 10 October

02
Heineken · Arsenal · Boat · Stacks
The big one
09:30
Breakfast — Louf (De Pijp) ☕
Michelin chef-backed bakery. Great pastries, great coffee. Chilled, trendy, not touristy. Right near Heineken.
10:30
Heineken Experience 🍺
Stadhouderskade 78. Interactive brewery tour, pour your own pint. ~1hr.
✓ Booked — deposit paid
11:45

13:30
Arsenal v Leeds — TBC bar ⚽
Kick off 12:30 BST / 13:30 Amsterdam. Somewhere with pool/snooker, big screen, smoke-friendly terrace. TBC — Gaffer is on it.
16:30
Hotel — showers, get ready
Game ends ~15:15 Amsterdam time. Back to hotel, freshen up before the evening.
17:30
Stripper boat 🛥️
Private canal cruise, 1.5hrs. Unlimited beer, wine & soft drinks. Special guest joins for the last 30 minutes. Back by ~19:00.
✓ Booked — Striptease Nederland
20:30
Dinner — Stacks Diner 🥩
American-style diner, Amsterdam-West. Set menu. James Dimitri and Top Jaw both rate it highly.
✓ Booked — 20:30, deposit paid
22:30
Coffeeshops + Red Light District 🌿
Rookies → Abraxas → Boerejongens or Jolly Good (Jordaan). Then Banana Bar, peep shows, wander De Wallen. The Therapist is on standby.

Sunday 11 October
